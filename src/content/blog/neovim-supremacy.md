## Introduction
Have you ever been given a 40-second pitch that sells you so hard your credit card was already in hand? I was. On a whim (well actually -- in the midst of doom-refreshing my YouTube to find something to numb the screaming sound of procrastination), I clicked on this short-form video displaying a few plugins on a bare-bones Linux OS. Effortlessly smooth, simple, responsive, yet also cut & clean, like an unobtainable romanticized NYC studio apartment straight out of a high-budget film. 

<i>Why, a mouse would be made entirely obsolete! My efficiency wouldn't falter anywhere. I'd be unchained from the shackles of my desk and mouse pad. No longer limited by the weights of my accessory tools.</i>
