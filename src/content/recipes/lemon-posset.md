---
title: "Lemon Posset"
description: "Three ingredients, no baking. Silky, tangy lemon cream that sets in the fridge."
category: "Dessert"
chillTime: "4 hrs – overnight"
cookTime: "10 min"
ingredients:
  - "3 lemons"
  - "600 ml heavy cream"
  - "180 g cane sugar"
steps:
  - "Zest the lemons (about 2½ lemons' worth)."
  - "Juice the lemons through a sieve."
  - "Simmer the cream with the zest and sugar for ~10 minutes. Use modest heat and treat it like boiling milk: look for bubbling, don't let it run away."
  - "At the edge of a simmer, pour in the lemon juice and stir fully."
  - "Pour through a sieve once more into lemon rinds, soufflé cups, or small bowls."
  - "Chill 4 hours to overnight. Longer is better."
tip: "Make it the night before: it sets firmer and tastes better."
---
